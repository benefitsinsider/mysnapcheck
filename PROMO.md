**Title:** Find your group under the new SNAP rules (free, 2 minutes)

**Post:**
The rules for keeping SNAP changed with the Big Beautiful Bill, and most people find out when their
EBT card stops working.

The time limit now runs to age 64. The exemption for parents now stops when the youngest
child turns 14. The automatic exceptions for veterans, people experiencing homelessness,
and former foster youth are gone.

So I built My SNAP Check. Five short questions put you in one of eight groups under the new
law. Then you get the yearly checklist for your group: income updates, recertification,
the interview, the periodic report, address changes, and the work rule if it applies to you.
You can print it or have it emailed.

Free. Nothing sold. We will never call you.

https://mysnapcheck.org/?src=skool

If you know which group you are in, drop the number in the comments. I will cover the
groups with the most questions in the next video.
