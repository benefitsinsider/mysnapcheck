# My SNAP Check — audit log

Every checklist line is written from a primary source (FNS, 7 CFR 273). Outside-model
cross-checks (ChatGPT + Gemini, browsing on) use `CROSSCHECK.html`. A dispute never wins on
its own; each one is settled at the primary source, and the stricter reading wins.

## 2026-10-05 — rebuild (v2, group checklists)

**Internal review before launch (all applied):**
- Groups 1–8 built from the FNS OBBB implementation memoranda: time limit now runs to age
  64; the dependent-child exemption now stops at 14; the veteran, homelessness and
  former-foster-youth exceptions were removed; a tribal-member exception was added.
- Ages 60–64: first built as exempt (group 1). **Corrected Oct 6, 2026** after the outside audit:
  FNS Q&A #1 (June 11, 2026) says 60–64 are under the time limit unless another exception applies.
- FY2027 figures (effective Oct 1, 2026) from the FNS COLA memo: max allotments, standard
  deductions, shelter cap $769, minimum benefit $25, gross limits 1:$1,729 … 8:$6,037.
- Reporting: a simplified-reporting household reports only when gross income passes 130%
  FPL (and ABAWD hours falling under 20/week) within 10 days after the end of the month;
  change-reporting households report any listed change within 10 days.
- Recertification: apply by the 15th of the last month of the certification period to keep
  benefits uninterrupted (7 CFR 273.14).
- Elderly/disabled households: no gross-income test, uncapped shelter deduction, medical
  deduction over $35/month, $4,750 asset limit.
